# Hypothesis

## H1 — Ordinary parent-state inheritance

REFR 02009742 follows enable-parent REFR 02017772 under the serialized XESP fields. The child and marker have no schema-defined Initially Disabled bit in the installed base records.

## H2 — Inverted or initially disabled state

The child's XESP bit 0 requests the opposite of the parent's state, or the child/parent REFR header sets Initially Disabled, adding a serialized barrier to ordinary parent inheritance.

These models concern static base fields. Neither model alone determines the loaded cell state, the collision geometry, whether the player can cross the rubble, or whether the stage-17 trigger fires.
