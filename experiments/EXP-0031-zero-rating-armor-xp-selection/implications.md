# Implications

- Do not treat a zero or negative displayed armor rating as evidence of zero armor XP. For the analyzed GOG build, eligibility reads positive base DNAM from worn ARMO entries; this is distinct from current effective rating ([EV-0222]).
- A genuine zero-DNAM item could avoid this eligibility predicate, but it remains a candidate only if separate defense is measured against a matched control during qualifying hits.
- The private lead screen does not establish Steam 1.6.1170.0 behavior or a player-reproducible exploit. Exact profile, disposable save, loaded instrumentation, raw progress, and damage/protection measurement remain prerequisites ([EV-0260]).
- No causal edge to OBJ-1 level-gated quest initiation or to unrelated AV39/ActiveEffect paths is supported.
