# Expected discriminants

- **H1, clothing rating contributes to protection:** the official record screen contains at least one positive-DNAM BOD2 type-2 ARMO, and a mapped writer for the PlayerCharacter armor-factor field incorporates that item's rating. The GOG receiver selector may still skip type 2. A matched Steam hit test must show lower incoming damage with exactly zero Heavy/Light AVSK change.
- **H2, clothing rating is excluded from this protection path:** no qualifying record exists, or the source that populates the armor-factor field excludes Clothing. A DNAM value by itself does not establish hit mitigation.
- **Evidence that would narrow but not decide:** a getter returning a cached field confirms only which value the hit builder reads. It does not show how worn items populate that field.
- **Live discriminator:** on verified Steam AE 1.6.1170.0 and a complete selected order, compare a naturally acquired candidate against an unarmored control under the same qualifying attacks. Record numeric pre/post health, Heavy and Light AVSK, and player level. No injected item, forced state, or altered plugin counts as vanilla reproduction.
