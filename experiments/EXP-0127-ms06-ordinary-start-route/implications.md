# Implications

- The level-10 Story Manager selection and an automatically enabled helper are separate source paths. A helper's existence does not establish that the main event quest can start from its stopped state.
- The GOG SetStage callback constrains the GOG 1.6.1179.0 native path and exact inspected QUST inputs. It cannot decide Steam AE 1.6.1170.0 behavior or a live alias/stage result.
- The helper's early appeal scene advances its owning helper quest, then the helper QF stages the separate rumor quest. Similar helper scene scripts do not themselves establish a main-quest start. `PotemaBossFightScene` has a main-MS06 stage-125 writer, but its natural scheduling remains unobserved; the configured main placed trigger requires an already-reached stage 50.
- The base-Misc-BSA miss for `defaultsetStageTrigSCRIPT` and `defaultsetstagealiasscript` is an extraction-route limit. It must remain an unknown, not a claim that those scripts are absent or inert.
- The level-one discriminator must separately observe MS06 running state, stage, Falk-note availability/read callback, alias fills, Potema/Wolfskull encounter state, and numeric player level, with a clean no-trigger control and replication.
- No shared call, ActorValue, HitData field, ActiveEffect, or serialization dependency to the OBJ-2 armor/XP route is currently supported; no cross-objective edge is added.
