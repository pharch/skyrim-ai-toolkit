# Hypotheses

- **H1 — interposition window:** a synchronous callback or player-accessible equipment transition can occur after mitigation and before the unblocked worn-ARMO scan, so the hit can be protected by armor but miss AV11/AV12.
- **H2 — same-pass resolver:** mitigation and the worn-armor AV11/AV12 decision execute synchronously in one resolver pass, with no player-input or yield point between them. Entry-point visitor work may update numeric hit values.
- **H3 — blocked-hit route:** a blocked hit can enter AV9 Block and skip AV11/AV12 after the protection stage; this is a separate control and does not alone satisfy the armor-XP prize condition.

The claims are build and source-scope hypotheses, not live-game outcomes. Exact predictions and experiment boundaries are in [protocol.md](protocol.md).
