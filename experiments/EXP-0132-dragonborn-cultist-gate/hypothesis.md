# Competing models

## Q83-H1 — explicit CLOC level gate

An explicit Player-level predicate in the screened official CLOC records or
loose PSC path prevents `DLC2CultistAmbush` from starting below a threshold.

**Prediction:** a `GetLevel` row or equivalent level read appears in the CLOC
parent/node/QUST, attached stage source, or a build-attested pre-start callback.

## Q83-H2 — main-quest state path; world gate is separate

The screened CLOC path depends on main-quest/event state and location, while
the level/location OR group belongs to the separate `DLC2WE09` world route.

**Prediction:** the CLOC branch selects its own QUST and its bounded records
and source fragments have no Player-level test; `DLC2WE09` remains a distinct
SCPT child with its own condition list.

## Q83-H3 — CLOC actor variants resolve through leveled templates

After event initiation, the CLOC aliases resolve their serialized `TPLT`
chain into player-level-dependent cultist actor variants.

**Prediction:** the alias chain reaches `LVLN` entries with player-level
selection flags, and a source/runtime path shows those entries resolve for
the actual CLOC aliases.

## Q83-H4 — serialized links do not drive CLOC actor selection

The CLOC alias `TPLT` links are stored but not used by the actual alias-fill
path; the runtime instead fills fixed NPC bases or selects actors through a
different route.

**Prediction:** the relevant alias-fill path skips the TPLT/LVLN chain, or a
natural level-one observation shows the fixed actor base despite the linked
leveled records.

Q83-H3 and Q83-H4 concern actor selection after the event. Neither is a
pre-start quest gate.
