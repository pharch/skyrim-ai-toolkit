# Competing hypotheses

## Q75-H1 — a fixed source exists in the official records

At least one Clothing ARMO whose ENCH/MGEF modifies a resistance ActorValue consumed by incoming spell or elemental damage is referenced by a fixed placement or guaranteed source in the exact official five-master records.

## Q75-H2 — no fixed source exists

The exact five-master records contain no fixed source for any screened candidate; identified sources are absent, leveled, or random.

## Q75-H3 — fixed placement exists but level-one acquisition remains unresolved

The item is placed in a quest/ownership-gated or otherwise inaccessible location, so a fixed record reference does not establish a deterministic physical level-one acquisition route.

## Shared limit

The source records can identify a route candidate and its conditions. They cannot prove physical access at character level one, correct profile winners, active enchantment behavior, measured protection, a qualifying hit, or zero Heavy/Light AVSK.
