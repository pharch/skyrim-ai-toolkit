# Implications

The conditional full HitData clear is controlled by the attacking actor's process/combat state. It can make receiver armor points zero, but it is a no-damage state that clears both physical and total damage; it does not show that armor caused the reduction or that useful armor protection is retained.

This narrows the Q-0025 exception and removes it as an armor-linked candidate on current evidence. A single bit-based direct setter remains unresolved and becomes Q-0027. Do not treat a skipped or zero-damage hit as a qualifying protected hit for OBJ-2.

No causal edge to OBJ-1 is supported. The shared PlayerCharacter skill sink remains unrelated to this attacker-side cancellation predicate.
