# Hypotheses

- **H1 — shared selector, non-positive points:** A pure magical hit reaches the shared player hit/armor resolver, selects the worn positive-DNAM Light Armor, and calls AV12 with zero or negative points. The PlayerCharacter `UseSkill` override rejects that amount while a resistance MGEF reduces incoming health damage.
- **H2 — selector bypass:** Pure magical damage does not enter the shared AV11/AV12 selector. No armor XP is therefore an ineligible-hit result, not XP suppression from a qualifying hit.
- **H3 — positive or alternate award / inaccessible candidate:** A magical route supplies positive points or awards AV11/AV12 elsewhere, or the candidate's official records/acquisition path fail the proposed protection and level-one conditions.

All three remain open pending the producer-backed GOG trace and exact record chain. GOG evidence is not a Steam runtime result.
