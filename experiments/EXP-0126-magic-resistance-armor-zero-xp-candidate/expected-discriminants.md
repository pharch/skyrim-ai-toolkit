| H1: shared selector with non-positive points | A build-attested magic producer reaches the same resolver/HitData receiver; the positive-DNAM item remains eligible, the resolver calls AV12 with a non-positive points value, and the PlayerCharacter guard returns. Exact ENCH/MGEF records feed the resistance check. | Proposed; candidate item and call path incomplete. |

A no-delta result alone cannot distinguish H1 from H2. Live confirmation requires an event known to qualify for the selector, evidence of protection, and raw Heavy/Light AVSK before and after the same hit.
