# Competing models

- **Q70-H1 — Fixed cave target:** WE13's aliases or spawn records identify a particular cave location, interior cell, entrance, or door.
- **Q70-H2 — Event-anchored encounter:** WE13 receives a Story Manager event reference/location, finds linked travel markers near that reference, and creates the encounter actors there.
- **Q70-H3 — Separate physical sites:** Trolls found in caves are ordinary world locations and need not be the source location for WE13's encounter.

H1 predicts a fixed dungeon location, cell, door, or placed troll reference in WE13's source record. H2 predicts event-filled aliases and linked references that determine encounter placement. H3 predicts multiple generic troll settings without a single cave identity.
