# Competing models

## H1 — level-12 Story Manager node is only one entry route

`DA09IncreaseLevelNodeSHARES` selects main DA09 after the level threshold, but a Beacon acquisition, statue interaction, or independent serialized startup/caller can start or advance DA09 without that node.

**Prediction:** the main QUST is independently startable, or its Beacon/statue callback starts it or sets an early stage without a level predicate; at least one item/interaction route is source-linked to that callback.

## H2 — ordinary quest starts remain level-gated

Beacon placement, pickup, and statue interaction may expose the item or voice line, but main DA09 startup/progression is gated at level 12 by QUST fields, dialogue conditions, scripts, or an equivalent predicate.

**Prediction:** no independent low-level start route is found in inspected QUST/VMAD/DIAL/INFO/REFR/leveled-list records and available PEX; start callbacks check level or depend on the gated event.

Neither absence in a bounded source screen nor UESP wording alone is sufficient to establish H2 or a level-one exploit.
