# Hypothesis

The DA07 Museum key may provide a player-accessible way through the keyed house boundary before the level-20 Story Manager start. The source lead is time-ambiguous: UESP lists KEYM `000960F0` as carried by Silus, but also says the house is inaccessible until level 20. Earlier official-master scans found the key in door/display-case lock fields and a QA-only container, not as a direct Silus inventory item. [EV-0267], [EV-0268], [EV-0269]

## Competing models

- **H1 — pre-gate inventory:** Silus's base inventory or an initially available leveled list supplies the key before DA07 starts; if Silus is accessible, level-one pickpocketing can unlock the door.
- **H2 — quest-conditioned key:** a quest stage, alias setup, package, or script supplies the key only after the level-20 route starts; UESP's inventory summary is not evidence of pre-gate availability.
- **H3 — static listing without a usable source:** the key is associated with Silus in a secondary item list, but installed source does not establish a player-accessible pre-gate holder or grant path.

None is a gameplay result. A base-record holder does not prove the actor is enabled, reachable, pickpocketable, or carrying the item at level one.
